# GapToGrowth Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2026

GapToGrowth is a free, open-source Claude plugin. This policy explains what it does with your information.

## What GapToGrowth reads and stores
- GapToGrowth reads the files you place in the folder you choose, such as your resumes and job descriptions. These contain personal details like your name, email, phone number, and work history.
- It writes files only in that same folder: your knowledge files, tailored resumes, job decision notes, Learning Hub lessons, and saved progress.
- Nothing is stored anywhere else. Delete the folder to delete everything.

## What GapToGrowth sends elsewhere
- Nothing. GapToGrowth has no server, makes no network requests, and does not collect analytics or usage data.
- The Learning Hub is a single local web page that runs offline. Its progress is saved in your browser and, when you choose to save it, in a file in your folder.
- The maintainer never receives your data.

## How Claude processes your data
GapToGrowth runs inside Claude. Your files and messages are processed by Claude under your own Claude account and Anthropic's terms and privacy policy. Some Claude features, such as Cowork sessions, may run in the cloud. See Anthropic's documentation for details.

## Other people's resumes
If you add friends' or colleagues' resumes as layout references, ask them first. GapToGrowth uses them only for layout ideas and never copies their content into your files.

## Children
GapToGrowth is not intended for people under 18.

## Changes and contact
Changes to this policy will be recorded in this file and the repository history. For questions, open an issue in the GapToGrowth GitHub repository. Please never include resume content or personal details in an issue.
