## Layout specs
- One page, US Letter, single column. No tables, text boxes, columns, icons, photos, or header or footer content.
- Margins: 0.7 inch on all sides.
- Font: Calibri. Body 11 pt. Name 16 pt, bold, all caps, centered.
- Contact line: centered, 10 pt, items separated by " | ".
- Section headings: 11 pt, bold, all caps, with a thin line underneath.
- Entry first line: organization or project in bold on the left, location or dates right-aligned on the same line.
- Entry second line: degree or title in italics on the left, dates in italics right-aligned.
- Bullets: 2 to 3 per entry. Start with an action verb, end with a measurable result.
- No summary section. Section order: Education, Experience, Projects, Skills, Leadership & Activities.
