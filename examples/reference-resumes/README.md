# Reference Resumes

Two fictional, one-page resumes that show the layout GapToGrowth considers best practice. GapToGrowth copies the PDFs into `Sample Resume/Reference Resumes` during onboarding and uses them for **layout ideas only**, never as facts about you.

| File | For | Section order |
|---|---|---|
| `Reference_Experienced_OnePage` (.docx, .pdf) | Professionals with several years of experience | Summary, Experience, Skills, Education, Certifications |
| `Reference_Graduate_OnePage` (.docx, .pdf) | Students and recent graduates | Education, Experience, Projects, Skills, Leadership & Activities |

## Standards they follow

- **One page, one column, reverse-chronological.** No tables, text boxes, columns, icons, photos, or header/footer content, so applicant tracking systems (ATS) can read them top to bottom.
- **Standard section headings** (Summary, Experience, Education, Skills) that ATS software recognizes.
- **Education first for graduates**, below experience for experienced candidates.
- **Calibri 11 pt body, 0.7 inch margins**, within the common 10 to 12 pt and 0.5 to 1 inch guidance. Name is the largest text; dates are right-aligned.
- **Bullets start with an action verb and end with a measurable result.** No "I", no "responsible for".
- **Contact details in the body**, not in the document header.

## Fictional data

Every name, company, school, and number is invented. Phone numbers use the 555-0100 to 555-0199 range reserved for fictional use in North America, and emails and websites use the reserved `example.com` domain. Any resemblance to real people or organizations is coincidental.
